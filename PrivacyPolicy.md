# ClearCalc Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

ClearCalc does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share calculations or personal data. Calculations are processed locally on the device and are not retained after the app is closed.

Hourly wage, split-group size, and any feedback you choose to save stay on the device and are not uploaded.

ClearCalc does not use accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, cookies, or third-party SDKs for profiling. The optional remote configuration service is limited to downloading a public announcement and an interface accent color from a developer-configured HTTPS endpoint. It cannot change calculations, introduce features, or access device data. If this service is enabled, the configuration host may receive ordinary HTTPS request metadata, such as an IP address and request time, in order to respond to the request.
